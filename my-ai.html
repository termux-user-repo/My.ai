What it's like

Not a tool you operate. A presence you return to.

Most software waits for instructions. my.ai carries context forward — your goals, your half-finished thoughts, the version of you that showed up yesterday — so every conversation starts further along.

It remembers

The project you're mid-way through, the name you mentioned once, the plan you're still deciding on. Nothing repeated twice.

It adapts

Your pace, your tone, how much detail you actually want. my.ai calibrates to you specifically, not to an average user.

It's honest

Real answers, real pushback when you need it — not agreement dressed up as help.
